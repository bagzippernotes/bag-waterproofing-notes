Water Resistance for Everyday Bags: What New Brands Should Decide

Customers often ask whether a bag is waterproof. The honest answer depends on several design choices. Here's how I explain them to founders.

Water-resistant vs waterproof

Most everyday bags are water-resistant: they shed light rain. Fully waterproof bags need sealed seams and special closures.

Fabric coatings

Coated fabrics repel water on the surface. Ask how the coating holds up after rubbing and folding.

Seams and zips

Water gets in through stitch holes and zips long before it soaks through fabric. Taped seams and covered zips help.

Base design

A coated or separate base panel keeps the bag dry when set down on wet ground.

Describe it accurately

Use clear wording on product pages so customers know what to expect in heavy rain.
